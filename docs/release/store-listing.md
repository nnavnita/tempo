## Full description — Play, 4000 chars

```
Most task apps give you a list. A list does not tell you when anything will
actually happen.

Tempo asks one extra question when you add a task — how long will this take —
and then does something with the answer. It reads your Google Calendar, finds
the gaps you are genuinely free, and puts the task in one. Your plan is made
of real times, not intentions.

WHEN YOU SLIP, IT RE-PLANS

Miss a block and most planners leave you with a plan that is quietly wrong.
Tempo notices, moves the work, and tells you. Nothing silently disappears.

And when something keeps slipping, Tempo stops pretending. After three misses
a task is flagged as stuck and asks you to decide: make it smaller, drop it,
or set aside time for it yourself. That is the difference between a plan and a
list of things you feel bad about.

NOTHING GETS LOST

If a task will not fit before its deadline, Tempo says so, and says why — no
gap big enough, or no gap left in time. Every task is always in exactly one
visible state. There is no bucket where work goes to be forgotten.

HOW IT WORKS

- Add a task with a duration, and optionally a deadline and priority
- Tempo reads your calendar and places it in your next genuinely free gap
- A reminder arrives when the block starts
- Mark it done, or let it slip and watch it get re-planned
- Long tasks can be split across several gaps

PRIVATE BY DEFAULT

Tempo is single-player. There is no feed, no sharing, and no other user can
see anything of yours. Only start and end times are read from your calendar —
never event titles, descriptions, locations or guests. No ads, no analytics,
no tracking.

WHAT YOU NEED

A Google account, so Tempo can read the calendar it plans around.
```
