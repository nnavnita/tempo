# Tempo — Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** 6 November 2026

Tempo is a personal planner. It reads your calendar to find free time and
places your tasks into it. This policy describes exactly what it collects and
why.

## What Tempo collects

**Your Google account details.** When you sign in with Google, Tempo receives
your name, email address and profile picture. These identify your account and
are not used for anything else.

**Your Google Calendar events.** Tempo reads events from your primary Google
Calendar to work out when you are busy. It stores a reduced copy — start and
end times only, with no titles, descriptions, locations or attendees — so that
planning still works when you are offline. If you let Tempo add planned work
to your calendar, it creates private events for those blocks.

**The tasks you enter.** Titles, notes, estimated durations, deadlines,
priorities, and the time blocks Tempo schedules for them.

**A notification token.** A device identifier issued by Firebase Cloud
Messaging, used to send your own reminders to your own device.

## What Tempo does not do

- No advertising, and no advertising identifiers.
- No analytics or behavioural tracking SDKs.
- Your data is never sold, rented, or shared with third parties.
- No other user of Tempo can see your tasks, your plan, or your profile.
  Access is restricted to your own account at the database level.

## Where your data is stored

In Google Firebase (Firestore and Firebase Authentication), operated by Google
Cloud. Data is encrypted in transit and at rest by the platform.

## How long it is kept

Until you delete it. Deleting a task removes it and its scheduled blocks.
Deleting your account removes your tasks, scheduled blocks, plan state and
cached calendar times.

## Your choices

- **Revoke calendar access** at any time in your Google Account permissions.
  Tempo will no longer be able to read your calendar, and planning stops.
- **Turn off notifications** in your device settings. Tempo keeps planning;
  it just stops reminding you.
- **Request deletion** by contacting the address below.

## Children

Tempo is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect
their data.

## Changes

Material changes to this policy will be reflected in the "last updated" date
and, where significant, surfaced in the app.
